Emmz's rulings on the whole-change review (`20261028-1700-whole-change-review.md`):
- **Refuse overlapping extras.** Nesting is not supported.
- **Build every proposed fix:** sections 10 and 11.

What changed:
- **10.1 / 10.2:** new `splitExtras(cfg, ws)` in `internal/cli/create.go`, shared by `planFor`
  (create and rebuild) and `runSync`. It checks, in order:
  1. each extra against the workspace;
  2. each pair of extras: refused when one lies within the other and either is `rw`;
  3. existence.

  Then it splits the extras into mounts and copies. Ro-inside-ro stays allowed because it can't touch
  the Mac. Callers add "; nothing was created" or "; nothing was synced". There's a new vm-access
  scenario, "Overlapping extras", and a README bullet.
- **Gotcha:** sync by name on a VM with no workspace mount passes `ws == ""`, and `Within(p, "")`
  matches every absolute path. `splitExtras` skips the workspace check in that case. Tested.
- **10.3:** `copyExtras` collects `writeTar`'s error from the goroutine. A Mac-side read error now wins
  even when the VM's tar exits 0. A plain `io.ErrClosedPipe` defers to the VM-side error. The test uses
  a `lenientTar` client wrapper to simulate tar accepting a short archive. Mutation check: the test
  fails against the old `copy.go`.

Self-review: the `ws == ""` false positive above was found and fixed. Nothing else found.
